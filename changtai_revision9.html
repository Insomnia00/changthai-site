Our Top-Selling Thai Dishes

D32. Traditional Pad Thai

Our undisputed best-selling Thai street food classic. Thin rice noodles stir-fried in our signature homemade tamarind sauce with free-range eggs, crushed peanuts, and fresh bean sprouts.

D42. Authentic Green Curry

A spicy, aromatic coconut milk curry infused with fresh green chilies, bamboo shoots, and sweet basil. A consistent favorite among our regular diners.

Grilled Pork Neck w/ Sticky Rice

Experience the true taste of Thailand with our perfectly marinated, tender grilled pork neck, served alongside traditional sticky rice.

D25. Cashew Nut Stir-Fry

A highly popular savory and slightly sweet stir-fry loaded with roasted cashew nuts, fresh vegetables, and your choice of protein.

Customize Your Dish Perfectly

We cook to your exact taste! Choose from 7 Spice Levels ranging from Level 1 (No Chilli) to Level 7 (Extra Hot).

Frequently Asked Questions

Where are you located?

We are conveniently located at 21 Shands Road, Hornby, Christchurch 8042. We have off-street parking available for our customers and are only 12 minutes from the airport.

Are you family-friendly and do you have high chairs?

Absolutely. We are a family-friendly restaurant and have high chairs available to ensure our youngest guests have a comfortable dining experience.

Do you offer vegetarian and gluten-free Thai food?

Yes! We have an extensive selection of vegetarian and vegan dishes, using fresh vegetables and tofu. Many of our curries and stir-fries are also naturally gluten-free.

Can I order Thai food delivery in Hornby?

Yes, you can order directly through our website via our online ordering system for the best pickup and delivery experience. We are also available for delivery on Uber Eats.
