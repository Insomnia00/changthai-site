A Taste of Thailand in Christchurch

Located at 21 Shands Road, Hornby, Chang Thai Restaurant is a beloved local destination for authentic, freshly prepared Thai food. Whether you are looking for a quick takeaway after work, planning a family dinner, or searching for the perfect dining spot near Christchurch Airport, our welcoming team is here to serve you. We blend time-honored Thai cooking techniques with the freshest New Zealand produce to bring you sweet, spicy, sour, and savory perfection.

Our Signature Thai Dishes

Traditional Pad Thai

Thin rice noodles stir-fried in our signature homemade tamarind sauce with free-range eggs, crushed peanuts, and fresh bean sprouts. A true Thai street food classic.

Authentic Green Curry (Gaeng Keow Wan)

A spicy, aromatic coconut milk curry infused with fresh green chilies, bamboo shoots, and sweet basil. Perfect for warming up on a chilly Christchurch evening.

Crispy Pork Belly (Moo Krob)

Our chef’s special crispy roasted pork belly, stir-fried with fresh vegetables, garlic, and chili in a rich savory sauce.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where are you located?

We are conveniently located at 21 Shands Road, Hornby, Christchurch 8042. We have off-street parking available for our customers.

Do you offer vegetarian and gluten-free Thai food?

Yes! We have an extensive selection of vegetarian and vegan dishes, using fresh vegetables and tofu. Many of our curries and stir-fries are also naturally gluten-free.

Yes, you can order directly through our website for both pickup and delivery via our online ordering system.
