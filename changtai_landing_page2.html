A Christchurch Culinary Gem Since 2019

Chang Thai Restaurant has been passionately serving what many consider the most authentic Thai cuisine in New Zealand. Here, the rich, bold flavours of Thailand come alive, crafted by our experienced Thai chefs who bring generations of tradition directly from their homeland to your table.

We believe a great meal is an experience. That's why we pair our exceptional food with a warm, cozy atmosphere and genuinely friendly, courteous service. Whether you're a local resident seeking your favourite Thai food or a visitor passing through our beautiful city, we are the perfect spot to experience the true taste of Thailand!

Our Signature Thai Dishes

Khao Soi (Northern Thai Curry Noodles)

Our highly recommended signature dish. A rich, coconut-based curry soup served with soft and crispy egg noodles, tender meat, and traditional accompaniments.

Classic Tom Yum

Experience the fiery kick of our classic Thai soup, expertly balancing spicy, sour, and aromatic flavors using authentic herbs and spices.

Massaman Curry

Experience the creamy comfort of a carefully simmered Massaman curry. A mild, deeply flavorful dish that is a staple of Southern Thai cuisine.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where are you located?

We are conveniently located at 21 Shands Road, Hornby, Christchurch 8042. We have off-street parking available for our customers and are only 12 minutes from the airport.

Do you offer vegetarian and gluten-free Thai food?

Yes! We have an extensive selection of vegetarian and vegan dishes, using fresh vegetables and tofu. Many of our curries and stir-fries are also naturally gluten-free.
