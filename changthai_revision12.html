About Chang Thai Restaurant

Founded in 2019, Chang Thai Restaurant has become Hornby and Christchurch's most beloved destination for authentic Thai cuisine. Our chefs bring generations of Thai culinary tradition directly to your table — from fragrant street-food classics to rich, slow-cooked curries.

We are proudly located at 21 Shands Road, Hornby, Christchurch 8042 — just 12 minutes from Christchurch International Airport, with free off-street parking.

Whether you're dining in with family, ordering takeaway, or getting delivery to your door, we ensure every meal is cooked fresh to order with the highest quality ingredients. We serve the communities of Hornby, Wigram, Prebbleton, Hei Hei, Sockburn and greater Christchurch.

Our Most Popular Thai Dishes

⭐ Best Seller

D32 · Traditional Pad Thai

Our iconic street-food classic — thin rice noodles stir-fried in our signature homemade tamarind sauce with free-range eggs, crushed peanuts, spring onions, and crisp fresh bean sprouts. Available with chicken, beef, prawn, tofu, or mixed.

Dietary: Gluten-Free | Vegetarian Option ✔

🌿 Customer Favourite

D42 · Authentic Green Curry

A deeply aromatic coconut milk curry infused with fresh green chilies, kaffir lime leaves, bamboo shoots, and sweet Thai basil. Rich, fragrant, and perfectly balanced between heat and creaminess.

Dietary: Gluten-Free | Vegan Option ✔

🔥 Chef's Special

Grilled Pork Neck with Sticky Rice

A true Thai street-food legend. Perfectly marinated, slow-grilled pork neck served alongside traditional glutinous sticky rice and our house dipping sauce. Tender, smoky, and unforgettable.

🥜 Popular Pick

D25 · Cashew Nut Stir-Fry

A crowd-pleasing savory and lightly sweet stir-fry loaded with roasted cashew nuts, fresh capsicum, mushrooms, onions, and spring onions in our special sauce. Choose your protein: chicken, beef, prawn, tofu, or mixed.

Dietary: Gluten-Free Option ✔

❤️ Local Favourite

Massaman Curry

A rich, mildly spiced coconut curry with tender slow-cooked meat, creamy potato, roasted peanuts, and warming spices like cinnamon and cardamom. One of Thailand's most celebrated dishes.

Dietary: Gluten-Free | Vegan Option ✔

🌶️ Spice Lovers

Thai Basil Stir-Fry (Pad Krapow)

A fiery, flavour-packed stir-fry of minced meat with fresh holy basil, garlic, and chili in a savory oyster sauce. Served with steamed jasmine rice and topped with a fried egg. An authentic Thai street-food staple.

🎛️ Fully Customizable — Your Way, Every Time

Choose from 7 spice levels: Level 1 (No Chili) to Level 7 (Extra Hot Thai Style). Remove any ingredient — peanuts, coriander, garlic, onions, or chili — to suit your dietary needs. All dishes available with Chicken · Beef · Prawn · Tofu · Mixed.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is Chang Thai Restaurant located?

Chang Thai Restaurant is located at 21 Shands Road, Hornby, Christchurch 8042, New Zealand. We have free off-street parking and are just 12 minutes from Christchurch International Airport. We proudly serve Hornby, Wigram, Prebbleton, Hei Hei, Sockburn, and all of greater Christchurch.

What are Chang Thai's opening hours?

We are open Tuesday to Sunday. Lunch is served 11:30 AM – 2:30 PM and Dinner from 4:30 PM – 8:30 PM. We are closed on Mondays. We recommend booking a table on weekends as we fill up quickly!

Does Chang Thai offer online ordering, pickup, and delivery?

Yes! You can place orders directly online at ordering.changthai.co.nz for both pickup and delivery. We also partner with Uber Eats for delivery across Hornby and surrounding Christchurch suburbs.

Does Chang Thai have vegetarian and vegan Thai food options?

Absolutely! We have an extensive selection of vegetarian and vegan Thai dishes using fresh vegetables and tofu. Many of our dishes are also naturally gluten-free. Every dish can be fully customized — just let us know your dietary requirements when ordering.

What is Chang Thai's most popular dish?

Our Traditional Pad Thai (D32) is our all-time best seller — thin rice noodles stir-fried in our signature homemade tamarind sauce with free-range eggs, crushed peanuts, and fresh bean sprouts. Other top favourites include the Authentic Green Curry (D42) and the legendary Grilled Pork Neck with Sticky Rice.

How spicy is Chang Thai's food? Can I choose my spice level?

We offer 7 customizable spice levels — from Level 1 (No Chili) all the way to Level 7 (Extra Hot, authentic Thai style). You can also request to remove any ingredients such as peanuts, coriander, garlic, or onions to suit your preferences.

Can I book a table at Chang Thai Hornby?

Yes! You can reserve a table easily online at booking.changthai.co.nz. We strongly recommend booking in advance, especially on Friday and Saturday evenings.

Is Chang Thai family-friendly? Do you have high chairs?

Yes! Chang Thai is a family-friendly Thai restaurant. We have high chairs available for young children and love welcoming families for a relaxed, authentic dining experience.

Is there free parking at Chang Thai Restaurant?

Yes, we have free off-street parking available directly at 21 Shands Road, Hornby, making it easy and convenient to visit us any time.

How long has Chang Thai been serving Christchurch?

Chang Thai Restaurant has been proudly serving the Hornby and Christchurch community with authentic Thai cuisine since 2019 — over 5 years of bringing the true flavours of Thailand to your table.
